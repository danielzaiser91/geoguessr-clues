# Latvia · Lithuania · Estonia

## Three-way table

Six clues that split all three at once. Any one of them is enough.

| Clue | LV | LT | EE |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Bollard** | thin, slightly curved | **wedge-shaped** | round, baton-like |
| **Chevron** | white, red arrows | white, red arrows **+ red outline** | **red, white arrows** |
| **km marker** | **parallel** to the road, number on one side | **45°** to the road, L-shaped sign | **perpendicular**, numbers on both sides |
| **Warning / speed sign border** | thick red **+ thin white outline** | thin red **+ thin white outline** | thin red, **no white outline** |
| **Guardrail reflector** | red or white | orange | **none** |
| **Pedestrian sign** | **5 stripes** | 3 stripes, low detail | 3 stripes, detailed (shoes, attached head) |

**Careful:** Estonian chevrons are identical to Russian and Ukrainian ones. Latvian and Lithuanian
chevrons are shared with Poland and much of Europe — only the red outline is Lithuanian.

## Direction sign arrows

Unique for LV. All three use white arrows on blue signs; the split is where the barb meets the
shaft.

| | Shaft → barb |
|---|---|
| **LV** | **angled**, slants back diagonally |
| LT · EE | straight, square to the shaft |

**Mnemonic:** Lat-via = an-gle. Two syllables each.

## Poles

| | Pole |
|---|---|
| **LV** | wooden, **hook-shaped insulators alternating left-right-left** |
| **LT** | **square concrete** with a diagonal support beam; poletops with short horizontal rods, sometimes trident-like |
| **EE** | least consistent of the three; round wooden is most common, often with a **small metal cap** on top |

**Careful:** hook poletops are rare but possible in the other two Baltics and elsewhere in Europe.
Square concrete poles turn up in EE too, rarely in LV. Metal caps also occur in LV and the Nordics.

**Mnemonic:** Latvian letters wear hooks — Ģ Ķ Ļ Ņ. So do its poles.

## Alphabet

Letters that appear in only one of the three.

| | Unique letters | Endings |
|---|---|---|
| **LV** | Ā Ē Ģ Ī Ķ Ļ Ņ | -s, -a |
| **LT** | Ą Ę **Ė** Į Ų Y | -as, -is, -us |
| **EE** | **Õ** Ä Ö Ü | – |

**Mnemonic — one city per mark:**

| City | Mark | |
|---|---|---|
| **Rī**ga | bar over i | LV |
| Klaip**ė**da | dot over e | LT |
| Ta**ll**i**nn**, V**õ**ru | doubled letters, õ | EE |

**Story:** Three Balts write a sentence. The Latvian draws a **bar**, the Lithuanian sets a
**dot**, the Estonian says everything **twice**. Over the **U** the Latvian and the Lithuanian
argue — so it stays **undecided**.

**Careful:** **Ū** = undecided. It exists in both LV and LT. Only the bar over **a, e, i** is LV.
