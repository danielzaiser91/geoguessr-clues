# Indonesia — regions

Only once you know it is Indonesia.

## Pine forest + angel's trumpet = highlands

**Angel's trumpet** (Brugmansia) — a shrub hung with large drooping trumpet flowers, cream to
apricot. It needs cool nights, so in Indonesia it means **mountains**, not lowlands. Documented
growing wild in the foggy pine forests at the foot of **Mount Slamet**, Central Java.

**Pine** (Pinus merkusii) narrows it further:

| | |
|---|---|
| Grows **naturally** | Sumatra only — **Aceh, Tapanuli, around Mount Kerinci** |
| **Planted** | all pine on **Java** is plantation, plus some on Sulawesi |

Together — pines, angel's trumpets, a narrow winding road, cool damp air — the most likely places
are the **volcano highlands of Java**: Slamet and Dieng, Bandung and Pangalengan, Garut,
Tawangmangu, Batu and Malang. Sumatra's highlands are the alternative: Karo, Toba and Tapanuli,
Kerinci, Bukittinggi.

Java is the better guess of the two: it has far more pine plantations and far denser coverage,
and plonkit notes coverage is sparse in Aceh.

**Careful:** the pine tells you highland, not which island. Confirm with a **kabupaten** name on a
sign or with the pole clues below.

## Pinning it down exactly

- **Kabupaten** ("Kab.") names appear on signs and billboards everywhere — 417 of them have
  coverage, and each one is a regency you can look up.
- **Postal codes** are five digits, grouped by first digit: **1xxxx Jakarta** rising eastward to
  **9xxxx Maluku and Papua**.
- **Cardinal directions** in place names: Utara north · Timur east · Selatan south · Barat west ·
  Tengah central · Tenggara southeast.
- **Satellite dishes** point roughly at central West Kalimantan. The direction tells you which
  side of the equator and which side of the country you are on; the angle tells you how far.

## Poles

| See this | Region |
|---|---|
| **Trident** poletop | **Central Java, Yogyakarta** — rarely S/W Kalimantan, N Sumatra, Riau |
| **Indent on both sides** | **West Java, Jakarta, Banten** |
| Extra long support on the poletop, sometimes two | **Lesser Sunda Islands** |
| Rectangle attachment on the poletop | almost only **Bali** |
| Horizontal text painted on the pole, usually dark blue | **Bali** |
| Even bar but **uneven insulators** — one side, two the other | almost only **North Sumatra** |
| Extra attachment right under the horizontal bar | **North and Central Sulawesi, Gorontalo** |
| Flat side of the crossbar facing **away** from the pole | **Sulawesi** — elsewhere it faces the pole |
| Thin black poles beside regular ones, two tapes near the top (light blue, green) | East Peninsula of **Central Sulawesi** |
| **A-shaped** support | **Kalimantan, Nusa islands** — but any bend in the wires can need one |
| Asymmetrical pole, two supports below the bar | **Sumatra**, southern Sulawesi |
| Bollard black at the bottom, white middle, yellow top | **Central Sulawesi** |
| Fences painted white with blue or red | **Sulawesi** |
| Green paint on bridge sides | **South and Central Kalimantan** |

## Landscape

| See this | Region |
|---|---|
| Oil palm plantations | **Sumatra, Kalimantan** — rare on Sulawesi |
| Rice | mostly **Java**, also South Sulawesi and South Sumatra |
| Dry, low, shrubby vegetation | the **Nusas** — but Java can look dry too |
| Very rounded green hills with palms, sugarcane or corn | **Gorontalo** |
| Savannah | northern **Sumba** |
| Red soil under a white layer, white spilling onto the road | **Bangka Belitung** |

## Roofs

| See this | Region |
|---|---|
| **Tiled** roofs | **Java**, sometimes southern Sumatra, Nusas, South Kalimantan |
| Plain **sheet metal** | north — Kalimantan, Sulawesi, Sumatra |
| Symmetrical triangle with two sloping levels | mainly **Sulawesi** |
| Horizontal slots inside the roof triangles | almost only **South Sulawesi** |
| **Crown-like roof pinnacles**, guardian statues, dark andesite stone | **Bali** |
| **Boat-shaped** roofs with spires, strongly angled | **West Sumatra** |
| Boat-shaped, flatter, no spires | **North Sumatra**, especially around Lake Toba |
| Roofs of **wooden chips** | **Kalimantan** |
| Gable **horns** | South Sulawesi, Riau, Central and South Kalimantan |
| **Tongkonan** — saddle roofs on stilted houses | around Rante Pao, **South Sulawesi** |
| Very high-pitched peaks | **Sumba** |
| Steep palm-leaf roofs | **Nias** |

## Miscellaneous

- **Churches and Christian signs** stand out in a Muslim-majority country — good clue for North
  Sumatra (Toba), Bali's neighbours, Sulawesi's Christian areas, and the Nusas.
- **Gen 3 antenna** coverage is mostly **Java**, most of all East Java; rare on Bali near Denpasar.
- **Black Gen 3** car is everywhere but most common on **Sulawesi**.
