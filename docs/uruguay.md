# Uruguay

**Prerequisite — rule these out first:** trident tops are not Uruguay-only. They also appear in
**Australia** (WA, and Victoria). The clue only decides once the continent is settled.

| Once you know | The trident says |
|---|---|
| You are in **South America** | **Uruguay** — AR and BR do not have it |
| You are in **Australia** | WA or VIC, not Uruguay |

**Trident pole** — pole top with **three insulators pointing straight up**. Uruguay uses several
pole types; this is the common one and it settles the AR and BR border regions.

**Mnemonic:** a **U** with a spike in the middle = trident → **U**ruguay.
