# Uruguay

**Prerequisite — the trident is not Uruguay-only.** It appears on four other continents, so the
clue decides nothing until the region is narrowed.

| Also has trident tops | |
|---|---|
| **Brazil — Rio Grande do Sul** | the neighbouring state, right across the border |
| **India** | trident on a square concrete pole is the **most common pole in the country** |
| **South Africa — KwaZulu-Natal** | almost unique to that province |
| **Australia — WA, VIC** | see [australia-states](australia-states.md) |
| **Lithuania** | some poletops are trident-like, one on top and two at the sides |

| Once you know | The trident says |
|---|---|
| South America, **not** Rio Grande do Sul | **Uruguay** — Argentina has none |
| South America, near the BR border | **undecided** — UY or Rio Grande do Sul |

**Trident pole** — pole top with **three insulators pointing straight up**. Uruguay uses several
pole types; this is the common one.

**Mnemonic:** a **U** with a spike in the middle = trident → **U**ruguay.

## Other Uruguay clues

| Clue | |
|---|---|
| **Triple road line** | double yellow with **white dashes between** — nowhere else in the Americas |
| Bollard | white, one side often painted yellow, plus stone barriers |
| Pole | round with flat metal sheet top, or square concrete |
| Guardrail support | three sides of a rectangle **plus corner bits of the fourth** — Argentina has only three |
