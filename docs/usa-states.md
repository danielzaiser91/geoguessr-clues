# USA — states

Only once you know it is the USA. Infrastructure only — landscape and architecture are not in
here yet, see [RESEARCH.md](../RESEARCH.md).

## Bridge and guardrail markers

| See this | State |
|---|---|
| **Yellow** sign with vertical **black triangles**, before bridges and at guardrail ends | **MN** |
| Small **green and red** rectangles at bridge and guardrail ends | **ME, NH, MA** |
| White metal sign reading **"E-2"** at bridge ends | **NH** |

**Mnemonic:** 10,000 lakes means countless river crossings — Minnesota marks every one.

## Bollards

| See this | State |
|---|---|
| Thin stick, **white field reflector with black outline** | **NV** |
| Dark post, **white circular** reflector with post sticking out **above** it | **CO, WY** |
| …and the post top painted **white or grey** | **WY** only |
| White or brown post, white rectangle front, two small squares back | **WA** |
| White **square** reflector on top of a **black rectangle** reflector | **UT** |
| White **diamond** reflector on top | **SD, MT** — MT usually 1 bolt, SD 2 |
| Very **long rectangular** reflector on top | **OR** — similar ones in CA |
| Black **paddle-shaped** body | **UT, WY, NE** — white square reflector on it means **WY** |
| **Three circular** reflectors | **NM** |
| Simple white bollard with black reflectors | **WI** |
| **Square** reflector | **UT** 2 bolts · **AZ** 1 bolt · **ID** either |

**Careful:** square and diamond reflectors are the same shape rotated. A tilted square looks like
a diamond, so read the bolts and the post before committing.
