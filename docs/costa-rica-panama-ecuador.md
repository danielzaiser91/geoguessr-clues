# Costa Rica · Panama · Ecuador

**Order — stop at first hit**

1. No antenna → **EC**
2. No front plate + green plate / green-blue top border → **PA**
3. Blur: all blurred except right side of truck front → **CR**
4. Flat terrain → **PA** (soft)

| Clue | CR | PA | EC |
|---|---|---|---|
| Antenna | yes | yes | **never** |
| Blur, right truck front visible | **yes** | – | – |
| Front plate | yes | **none** | yes |
| Plate colour | – | **green / green-blue top border** | – |
| Terrain | hilly | **flatter** | hilly |

Antenna splits EC from the rest. Plates split PA from CR.
