# Costa Rica vs. Panama vs. Ecuador

Three countries that look alike from a random road: tropical, mountainous, similar vegetation,
similar road furniture. Almost every round is decided by the camera car and by plates.

## Decision order

Work down this list and stop at the first hit.

1. **Antenna on the car?**
   - No antenna → **Ecuador**
   - Antenna → Costa Rica or Panama, continue
2. **Front plate missing, plate green or with a green/blue top border?** → **Panama**
3. **Everything blurred except the right side of a truck front?** → **Costa Rica**
4. Still stuck: **flat terrain favours Panama**, hilly favours the other two.

## Car

The strongest tool here, mainly because it isolates Ecuador.

### Antenna — unique for Ecuador

Ecuador **never** has an antenna. Costa Rica and Panama do.

So: no antenna is a positive ID for Ecuador. An antenna only tells you it is *not* Ecuador — you
still need plates or the blur to split CR from PA.

### Blur pattern — unique for Costa Rica

Costa Rica has a car where the whole front is blurred **except the right side of the truck front**,
which stays visible. If you see that combination, stop looking.

## License plates

The strongest tool for Panama.

- **No front plate.** Panama does not require one, so cars come at you with a bare bumper.
- **Green plates**, or plates with a **green or blue top border**.

Both together are close to conclusive. The missing front plate alone is worth a lot on its own,
because oncoming traffic gives it to you for free without needing to read anything.

## Landscape

Soft clue, use it only to break a tie.

**Panama is flatter** than Costa Rica and Ecuador. Long stretches of flat road with no relief in
the background lean Panama; constant hills lean CR or EC.

## Summary table

| Clue | Costa Rica | Panama | Ecuador | Strength |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Antenna | yes | yes | **never** | Unique for EC |
| Blur, right truck front visible | **yes** | — | — | Unique for CR |
| Front plate | required | **not required** | required | Strong for PA |
| Plate colour | — | **green, or green/blue top border** | — | Strong for PA |
| Terrain | hilly | **flatter** | hilly | Soft |
