# Australia — states

Only once you know it is Australia.

| See this | State |
|---|---|
| White property marker on a black post at the roadside | **SA** |
| **Stobie pole** — two rails with a slab between them ("sandwich pole") | **SA** |
| Metal pole full of holes | **NT** |
| **Flat** sign post — the post, not the sign | **SA or WA** only |
| **Yellow** sign post | **WA** — mostly, not always |
| **Yellow** licence plate | **NSW** |
| **Red** "No Stopping" sign | **NSW** — only state |
| **White** direction sign | **TAS** |

**Careful:** SA uses **rounded** posts too. A round post never rules SA out — only a flat one
rules the other states out.

**Mnemonic:** flat post = the two "-A" codes, S**A** and W**A**. Yellow **plate** = east (NSW),
yellow **pole** = west (WA).

## WA utility poles

| On the pole | |
|---|---|
| **Trident** top | WA — **also VIC** |
| **Green** painted bottom | WA |
| Sticker: **red/white**, **yellow horizontal**, or **white/grey** | WA |

**Careful:** the trident top alone leaves WA and VIC open. Green paint or a sticker decides it.

Marker, post and pole clues: [plonkit.net/australia](https://www.plonkit.net/australia).
