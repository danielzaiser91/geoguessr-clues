# Canada — provinces

Only once you know it is Canada.

## Signs

| See this | Province |
|---|---|
| T-intersection sign with a **rectangular yellow border** around the diamond | **NL** |
| T-intersection sign **without** the chequerboard pattern | **QC** |
| Stop sign reading **"Arrêt"** only | **QC** |
| Stop sign reading **"Stop" and "Arrêt"** | **NB** mostly |
| **Red-and-white** chevron | **QC** — the rest of Canada uses yellow-and-black |
| **Green** backside on road signs | **BC** (fairly common on **PEI** too) |
| White **triangles** on the signpost | **SK** |
| Green-and-white **striped sticker** on the signpost | **MB** |
| Yellow **fire hydrant marker** on top of the signpost | **QC** (also Edmundston, NB) |
| km marker with a **white square at the bottom** | **ON** |
| Speed sign with a **black band** reading "km/h" | **ON** — BC writes km/h without the band |

**Mnemonic:** Newfoundland puts a **frame** around the diamond.

## Bollards

| See this | Province |
|---|---|
| Cylindrical, two **thin** black bands | **AB** — the most frequent Canadian bollard |
| Cylindrical, two **thick** black bands | **NT** |
| **Thin, slightly curved**, black and white | **BC** |
| Black and grey, **orange stripe** below the grey | **MB** |
| Long thin white pole **attached to the guardrail**, red or green reflector | **QC** |

**Careful:** AB, NT and BC all look like a white post with two black bands. Band thickness splits
AB from NT; the thin curved shape and wider band spacing split BC from AB.

## Poles

| See this | Province |
|---|---|
| Transformer **faces the road** | **BC, NB** — elsewhere it sits parallel to the wires |
| Transformer at a **45° angle** | **PEI, NS** — red numbers on the side means PEI |
| Insulators face **toward or away from** the road | **QC, NS, PEI** |
| Two side insulators with a distinct attachment, one on top | **ON** |
| Insulators **alternate pole to pole** — 1 left/2 right, then 2 left/1 right | **SK** |
| Two **hanging** insulators | **MB** |
| Vertical black-and-yellow label with a small **Q** | **QC** |
| Small **white sticker** | **PEI** |

## Rural road numbering

| | |
|---|---|
| **AB** | **Twp Rd** runs east-west, numbers rise going north · **Rge Rd** runs north-south, numbers rise going west |
| **MB** | Number + direction. **N** = miles north of the US border · **E/W** = miles from the meridian just west of Winnipeg |
