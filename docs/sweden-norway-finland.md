# Sweden · Norway · Finland

## The M sign

Blue sign with a large white **M**, on smaller roads.

| | |
|---|---|
| **SE** | normal M |
| **NO** | same sign, different font — the **middle point of the M reaches down to the baseline** |
| **FI** | no such sign at all |

**Mnemonic:** the Norwegian M **sags to the ground**.

## Sweden only

| See this | |
|---|---|
| Wooden electricity marker, **blue and white stripe at the top** | found in no other Nordic country |
| Signpost stripes in **red and yellow**, blue and red, or white and red | blue-and-white also occurs in FI and EE |
| Road works, road blocks, even chevrons painted **red and yellow** | |
| Church flag: red cross on yellow with a **crown** | |

**Mnemonic:** Sweden signs in its own flag colours — yellow on blue.

**Careful:** the patterned highway outer line also occurs in **Denmark**, where the individual
sections are rectangular. And yellow-on-blue chevrons exist in **Luxembourg**, though very rarely.
